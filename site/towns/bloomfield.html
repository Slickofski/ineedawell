What the snapshot covers for Bloomfield

Built for your lot, not the town average.

Permits: new private wells in Bloomfield are permitted through the West Hartford-Bloomfield Health District. A well permit typically runs about $200 via the health district; verify with the district when the time comes.

What we can say statewide (not Bloomfield-specific): Connecticut's open well-completion dataset holds 8,205 reports: online submissions since January 2021. Older records are fragmented across towns, health districts, and unscanned files. Planning cost rates run $25 to $65/ft (2026 national guide ranges, planning context only), and every snapshot carries a +$10,000 to $25,000 dry-hole contingency, because the standard driller contract bills the attempt whether the hole comes up wet or dry.

Sources: CT DCP open well data; 2026 published national guide ranges.

Water quality: Connecticut requires arsenic and uranium testing for newly constructed private and semipublic wells (CT DPH, effective October 1, 2022). The snapshot flags what your area's geology suggests testing for: a lab test on your own well still has the final word.

Setbacks (CT top-lines): the legal distance a well must sit from septic and water: 75 ft well-to-septic at under 10 gallons per minute (gpm) (the band follows the well's required withdrawal rate, not its tested yield), 25 ft from a watercourse. Your snapshot lists the top-lines to confirm for your lot with the health district.

We don't publish town-wide averages: depth and yield vary lot by lot even within Bloomfield. Request a snapshot for your exact address and we pull the records around it.
