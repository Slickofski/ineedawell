Wells here were sealed: check for a street main before budgeting

No water-supply wells in the online record (filings since 2021) for this 13.3-acre tract, but the record is not empty: 5 of the filings near this parcel are well abandonments, and town-wide in Windsor the record shows 47 abandonments against 0 water-supply wells. The filings don't say WHY those wells were sealed: replacement wells, demolitions and public-water hookups all produce abandonment records. A public main arriving is one possible explanation, so check whether public water serves this lot (water company, health district) before budgeting for a well at all. It matters beyond convenience: under CT rules (Public Health Code §19-13-B51m), a well permit can be barred when a parcel boundary sits within 200 ft of an approved public water supply. At tract scale that can change the whole approval picture: public water at the street may serve the lots, or bar wells lot by lot.
